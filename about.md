I'm a software architect, grounded in hands-on development and mechanical sympathy. But my interests extend well beyond software. I'm unreasonably curious about how things work. That includes pretty much everything. I have a habit of trying to make sense of the world by breaking things down into models, rules and systems (<span style="color: #8a8a8a;">sometimes</span> to the displeasure of my loved ones). I enjoy exploring ideas, challenging assumptions and delving into counterfactuals. And, occasionally, turning my thoughts into something tangible.

This website is a place to collect and share some of those thoughts, ideas and projects. Perhaps something here will be useful to someone, or encourage them to explore an idea of their own. Admittedly, putting things on the internet gives me a satisfying sense of permanence, too (if only!).

Thanks for stopping by,<br>
Måns Tegling
