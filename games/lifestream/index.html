Tend the lifestream.

Begin lives around the world ring. Follow the moving cloud, shelter energy through living people, and help the world endure the disturbance.

Plant with care. An embryo needs 12 energy within 6 seconds. Seeds are unlimited. Failed embryos destroy all energy they absorbed.

A few quiet rules

Watch where life can grow.

Tap the inner ring to plant. Move the pointer, or use the arrow controls, to inspect the supply at a location. Planting closes six seconds before gathering.

Souls fill gradually. Embryos absorb nearby energy until they hold 12. The center fills as energy arrives; the outer arc shows time left. A filled embryo still develops for six seconds. Failed embryos destroy what they absorbed. Planting too many can make all of them fail.

Space enriches life. People create more energy when fewer others share their surroundings. Their glow reflects that growth; everyone still has a finite lifetime.

Violet means a sink. Dashed countdowns warn of arrival. Color drains in the final moment before activation. A flash marks activation; the black disk consumes cloud energy and the violet arc shows time left. The cloud moves clockwise, faster near the outside.

After the final ring, calm returns. The remaining people rise into the cloud, keep their remaining energy, and count as completed lives. Failed embryos do not count.

Your time is yours. Space pauses; switching tabs pauses automatically. Art view hides the interface and continues at the current pace; Esc, F, or double-click exits.
