A few quiet rules

Seeds have six seconds to gather a soul and develop. Failed seeds lose the energy they absorbed. Seeds are unlimited; energy is shared.

Every 150 energy created earns a lasting sanctuary. Tap to choose a location, or drag the Sanctuary button onto the world. Overlapping sinks are neutralized.

Planting closes six seconds before gathering. Every remaining person then returns to the cloud and counts as a completed life.

Use ← → to choose a location and Enter to plant, or use the Plant button. Space pauses, F opens Art view, and Esc leaves it. Switching tabs pauses automatically. Desktop players can right-click or Control-click to deploy a sanctuary.
