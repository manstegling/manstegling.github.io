**Lifestream** started with a thought: if reincarnation is real, where do all the extra souls come from as the population grows? I started designing a dynamic system to explore this question, and realised it could actually make for a fun game. So I introduced a disaster: an experiment gone horribly wrong, causing soul-eating wormholes to appear. Your job is to guide humanity through the crisis. The code is mostly AI-generated, but the fun isn't.

[Play Lifestream]({{ '/games/lifestream/' | relative_url }}) — a run takes a few minutes, on desktop or mobile.

<!--end_intro-->

Embryos require soul energy to grow into people. Nearby people make starting a new life easier, while open space gives each successful life more room to grow. Temporary wormholes swallow soul energy travelling between the two planes. The challenge is knowing when to expand, when to wait, and when to begin again.

The goal is to create as much new soul energy as possible before time runs out. Your first placement starts the clock, and you can pause whenever you like.

This is a playable prototype, and I'm still experimenting with mechanics, balance and pacing. I will publish more information about the game mechanics and the thinking behind it soon.
