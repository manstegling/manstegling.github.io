I’m currently designing a family-friendly strategy board game “Project Lumen” for 2-4 players. The idea is to make something that feels epic and wonderful, yet is dead-simple to play and doesn’t take forever to play. Unlike traditional strategy games, skill will only give you a marginal advantage, so kids or inexperienced player still will win from time to time.

The board is based on flat-top hexes. The full board (4 players) is approximately 13 rows x 15 columns and contains permanent structures such as towns, mines, landmarks and a dragon’s lair. It also holds event markers, which disappears upon activation and gives the activating player an event card.

To prototype map layout I’ve built a map editor tool (with the help of AI). Feel free to [try Lumen Map Editor]({{ ‘/tools/lumen-map-editor/' | relative_url }}). It runs completely in-browser and makes no external calls.
